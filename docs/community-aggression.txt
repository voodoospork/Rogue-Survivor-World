Community aggression
====================

Communities have three saved aggression policies in addition to their home,
property and feud rules:

- Guarded communities use the existing warning, restitution and feud behavior.
- Outsider killers initiate attacks on visible human outsiders without needing
  theft, trespass or a prior feud.
- Raiders attack vulnerable outsiders for possible supplies when their ordinary
  food reserve is low, or when wounded without healing medicine.

Raider decisions use visible wounds, equipped weapons and nearby opposition.
They cannot inspect hidden backpacks, ammunition counts or remote companions.
Police retain their previous rules. Own residents are excluded as targets;
player followers, orders, relocation and active organized raids exclude
voluntary predation. Ordinary combat, emergency needs and personal judgment
remain in force. A policy creates neither a global feud nor remote knowledge.

An unarmed civilian community member will not initiate an attack on a visibly
armed human. During an existing fight or hunt, the member retreats; if trapped,
the member waits instead of punching the armed opponent. This also applies to
ordinary community feuds. Equipped melee weapons, loaded firearms and grenades
count as ready weapons. The opponent's equipped firearm looks armed regardless
of its hidden ammunition. Backpack weapons are not detected. Two unarmed
survivors may still fight when the ordinary policy and opportunity checks allow
it. Equipment decisions still happen first; personal player orders retain their
existing authority.

Predators use ordinary aggression: victims can defend themselves, killing an
innocent survivor remains murder, and actual witnessed violence affects later
diplomacy. Raiders use real ground pickups, not generated rewards. They walk to
the target's last observed position to collect usable ordinary food or healing
medicine, excluding entrusted cargo. Capacity, ownership, theft responses and
ordinary death drop/destruction rules still apply; a hunt may yield nothing.

A hunt lasts at most 40 personal turns. Loot, serious injury, sleepiness,
starvation, elapsed time, an empty searched location or eight blocked travel
decisions cause withdrawal. The return has a 60-turn/eight-blocked-decision
limit. A half-world-day personal cooldown prevents repeated robberies.
Withdrawal does not erase personal hostility. Unseen relocation never updates
the remembered target position. Organized shelter raids still require known
feuds and dated shelter reports.

Policies are stable after initialization. Founder traits determine the existing
enforcement culture; gentle cultures and police stay guarded. Other identities
can produce raiders or outsider killers, with stronger enforcement favoring
predation. Actual world frequency depends on founding, survival and opportunity.

Predatory groups use a stable welcoming, neutral or wary cover attitude in
introductions and outsider conversations. Their stated home rules follow that
cover. Different residents and saved games retain the same cover for the same
community. When able to greet a new player contact, a predator can give one
ordinary introduction before attacking on its following decision. The shared
contact cooldown prevents repeated introductions from postponing combat.
Introductory speech and V reports do not confess their hidden policy
or retell private policy notes. Journal entries store the spoken claim, not the
hidden culture. Membership, actual property enforcement and attack decisions
still use the true policy. An actual attack can reveal intentions, and a later
reassuring claim cannot erase a threat already heard. True internal briefings
are deferred around visible outsiders in hearing range. A concealed listener
can still overhear them; observation and third-party warnings remain evidence.

Spoken attack threats name the community. Nearby awake listeners can learn that
threat through the existing dated community records. No new key is required.
Save format 5 is retained through optional fields. Older saves create
no hunt, people or supplies; old cultures initialize the missing policy during
ordinary play. Foreign reports stay stale until new information is heard.

Responsive NPCs does not continue quiet routines for predators or hunts, and
Adaptive World keeps awake predators and hunts detailed so encounters and hunt
deadlines cannot disappear into quiet catch-up.

World 0.2.0 validation, October 20, 2026
---------------------------------------
Release has zero warnings/errors. Native units and all 87 focused checks pass,
covering the new behavior, existing culture/clarity/home/raid cases, real first
contact followed by aggression, saved cover stories, evidence retention,
unarmed/armed decisions, disarmed pursuit, trapped defenders, distant Adaptive
safety, version metadata and genuine legacy format-5 loading.

The final full regression is 575/578. Generated diplomacy cannot complete its
scripted V interaction, the overnight community leaves the expected encounter
map, and the scavenging beneficiary dies during the return. These journeys have
historical failures, but their current outcomes are not causally isolated or
declared equivalent to older failures. A clean full-suite pass is not claimed.
The large and copied-save cases pass the unchanged 10-second/50,000,000-byte
limits. Initial fixture and home-interaction failures are retained separately;
the corrected home cases pass without relaxing their gameplay assertions.
No new population-performance gain or human playtest is claimed for 0.2.0.

World 0.3.0 update - October 22, 2026
-------------------------------------
Raider communities now prefer an actual robbery demand and independent answer
before starting a supply hunt. Desperation and visible gear influence the risk;
chiefs can also demand daily food tribute from weaker communities at contact.
The current rules and final validation are in robbery-and-tribute.txt.
