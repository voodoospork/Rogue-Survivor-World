Robbery and community tribute - World 0.3.0
===========================================

Robbery controls
----------------
V, then 7: Rob an adjacent awake human. Choose food (up to two units), healing
medicine (one unit), or the equipped weapon. A pending ordinary request also
accepts 7. Escape cancels without spending a turn. Existing bindings remain.

A demand costs one ordinary action, inflicts no damage, and gives the victim
self-defense. An NPC independently surrenders or refuses. If you are the victim,
V offers Y to surrender available requested goods, N to refuse, or Escape to
choose another action. You may fight or flee instead. The response window lasts
eight world turns, expires on separation, and is interrupted by injury/combat.
Only consent transfers your inventory. A robbery never reveals your backpack.

Real items change hands, preserving quantities, weapon state and food expiry.
Entrusted freight, spoiled food and non-healing medicine are excluded. Capacity
can prevent or limit a transfer. A partial surrender settles that personal demand;
a full inventory or missing goods cannot create loot. Successful surrender ends
only the newly created personal threat, earns no aid/trust reward, and retains
witnessed coercion. Prior crimes, faction hostility and community feuds remain.
The same robber cannot issue another demand for half a world day.

Raider behavior
---------------
Community raiders now approach suitable visible outsiders and threaten them
before attacking. Hunger, starvation, low personal food, wounds without medicine,
visible weapons, armor, wounds and nearby opposition affect opportunity. Greater
desperation permits greater risk. Own loaded weapons are required against armed
victims; an opponent's equipped firearm looks armed regardless of its unknown
ammunition. Hidden gear, stock and remote companions are not inspected. Victim
courage, personal shortages, visible force and known nearby support affect refusal.

Normal immediate explosive danger, equipment choices, player-party exclusions,
personal orders and survival rules remain. Raiders with food to eat do not rob
merely because they are hungry. Outsider-killer communities retain their separate
attack policy. A refused or unanswered visible demand can start the existing
bounded hunt, real combat, physical loot collection and withdrawal. If the odds
have become unfavorable the robber can back down. An unseen fleeing target
produces no new tracking destination. These decisions do not promise safety.

Tribute
-------
A raider chief can approach a visible, identified weaker chief and demand daily
food tribute. At least one observed community supporter is required. Counts and
equipment are limited to nearby awake people whose affiliation the observer
knows. No distant roster, enemy backpack or remote stock is inspected. Chiefs
with personal orders, player-party duties, relocation, raids or personal hunts
cannot negotiate. Police keep their existing organization and rules.

Demands specify two to four food units per world day. The receiving chief can
agree or refuse based on visible force, courage and observed household starvation.
Acceptance allows 180 world turns for the first collection, including partial
payments, while keeping one usable food unit for the paying chief. Only the
chief's carried personal food is available. An impoverished household can seek
food during that grace period. A full collector does not repeatedly consume the
payer's turns with zero transfers.

Each installment is physically handed over at adjacent contact. Completing it
schedules the next due date one day later. Unpaid balances do not grow through
remote deductions or compounded missed-day taxes. The community accounts remain
independent, bounded to four each, and are exchanged only at contact. Succession
retains the community's own account. An absent or unseen community is never
silently taxed, declared dead, or inferred to have refused from its live stock.

Pending talks and accepted tribute suspend unprovoked predation between the
participants. Payment buys that community truce, without erasing personal
hostility or other crimes. A forceful, still stronger collector may publicly
declare a feud after actual refusal or failed collection at contact. Existing
normal feud/raid rules then apply. Twelve unanswered turns retire an initial
demand at contact without automatically treating silence as refusal. These are
local encounters and collections, not a remote taxation or courier system.

Player information and compatibility
-----------------------------------
V / 4 (Relations) / 5 reports a chief's dated tribute accounts. Overheard demands,
agreements and collections can enter existing community news and the J journal.
Reports preserve dates and sources; they do not grant access to the live ledger.
Threats can generate attributed diplomatic grievances only through participants,
actual witnesses and later reports. No player government authority is added.

Optional serialized fields retain format 5, assembly identity 0.4.1.0 and inherited
save/mod compatibility 0.4.1. Introduced in World 0.3.0. Responsive NPCs and
Adaptive World retain detailed handling for pending coercion and tribute accounts.
No people, supplies, gear or permissions are generated by loading an older save.
