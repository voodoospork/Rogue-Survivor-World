Survivor activity / World 0.3.1
==============================

Survivors keep their ordinary gathering and trading while becoming familiar
with neighbors. Familiarity is still earned through actual observed turns or
useful cooperation; membership still needs a proposal and an independent answer.

Shared-refuge movement is a low-priority activity. It needs a viable claim with
sleeping space, keeps the survivor exploring near a visible partner, and never
returns a social wait. Shared-refuge movement precedes personal leader recruitment,
so gaining followers cannot silently prevent independent community formation. A failed social opportunity stops guiding movement after
four game hours of actual shared observation. A cached shelter check is refreshed hourly or when changing
buildings, so idle acquaintance does not flood-fill the map every turn.

Residents who are not on watch can use normal exploration and gathering at home
and nearby. An idle outing lasts at most two game hours and stays within twelve
tiles of the home anchor on that map. Its next home decision starts a return
when time or distance is exceeded, or on another map. Returning continues all
the way to the claim rather than oscillating at the radius. Active tasks,
combat, urgent survival, personal parties and explicit orders retain priority.
Watchers still use real posts and ordinary alarm/rotation rules.

Saved optional AI fields retain outing destination identity, deadline and return
state. Existing saves default to an ordinary return if already away; no new game
is required. Save format 5, compatibility 0.4.1 and assembly identity 0.4.1.0 are
unchanged. Shelter-check caches are not serialized.

Validation - October 24, 2026
-----------------------------
Windows Release builds with zero warnings/errors and passes native units. All
seven new activity cases pass in Classic, Responsive NPCs and Adaptive World.
The installed executable passes native units and 14 focused checks covering
activity, formation/refusal, home/watch/orders and genuine pre-community saves.

Full regression records 622/624. It retains a generated diplomacy player death
and follower reunion driver failure. The corrected follower driver separately
passes its complete 197-round journey, including reload and district travel;
the earlier full result remains qualified. All robbery/tribute and stair cases
pass, as do overnight membership and the unchanged save/load budget checks.
Large-fixture saves take 8.200 / 7.930 / 7.771 seconds, cold loads take
6.166 / 6.222 seconds, and the file is 9,996,297 bytes, within 10 seconds / 50 MB.

The repeated generated 50x50 initial-decision sample records 0/67 waits, compared
with 22/67 on the preserved 0.3.0 baseline. This samples one decision per civilian
across three seeds, not a long-term scheduler or natural-frequency measurement.
Longer generated trials produce 0/3/1 tribes across 1080/1010/125 rounds; the
last two end in ordinary player death. Formation varies and safety is not assured.

Evidence is retained outside the source repository under
working/community-validation/startup-activity/. Human playtesting remains open
for outing frequency and settlement survival. Linux/Docker were not verified
locally for this release.
